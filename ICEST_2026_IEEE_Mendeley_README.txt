ICEST 2026 – Custom Mendeley CSL

Based only on the reference examples supplied in the ICEST 2026 template.

Matched features:
- Numeric in-text citations: [1]
- Multiple citations: [1], [2]
- Consecutive citation ranges: [1]–[3] (processor-dependent dash rendering)
- References numbered by order of citation
- Authors as initials + family name
- 'and' before final author, without an Oxford comma
- Article title in double quotation marks
- Journal / conference title italicized
- Journal format: Vol. X, Issue: Y, pp. X–Y, Mon. Year
- Conference format: conference title, event date, place, Vol., pages, year
- Book title italicized and in double quotation marks

Important:
The ICEST template itself contains small inconsistencies among its five examples
(e.g., comma placement around Vol./Issue and page-range spacing). This style uses
one consistent form matching the dominant examples.

CSL cannot force Word font size (the template requests 9 pt for references) or
Word paragraph justification. After generating the bibliography in Word, set:
Times New Roman, 9 pt, justified, single spacing.

For conference references, enter Event Date and Event Place in the Mendeley
record if you want the full conference-date/place pattern.
